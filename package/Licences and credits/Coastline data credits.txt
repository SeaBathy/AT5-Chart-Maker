COASTLINE DATA IN program\coastlines
====================================

world_coast.land
----------------
GSHHG - A Global Self-consistent, Hierarchical, High-resolution Geography Database, version 2.3.7
by Paul Wessel (University of Hawaii) and Walter H. F. Smith (NOAA).
https://www.soest.hawaii.edu/pwessel/gshhg/
Used: the full-resolution shoreline polygons, level 1 (land) and level 5 (Antarctic ice front),
packed into AT5 Chart Maker's compact .land format (coordinates rounded to 1e-7 degree).
GSHHG is distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 3 or later (see
"GSHHG LICENSE.TXT", "lgpl-3.0.txt" and "gpl-3.0.txt" in this folder).

australia_coast.land
--------------------
GEODATA COAST 100K 2004 - national coastline and island polygons at mean high water, 1:100 000.
Copyright Commonwealth of Australia (Geoscience Australia) 2004.
Source: https://pid.geoscience.gov.au/dataset/ga/61395 (61395_shp.zip, layer australia/cstauscd_r)
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), see "cc-by-4.0.txt":
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes made: polygons coded "sea" were removed and the rest packed into the compact .land format
(coordinates rounded to 1e-7 degree). The coastline is from 2004 mapping.

Neither data set is for navigation.
